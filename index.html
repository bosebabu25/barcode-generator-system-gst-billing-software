Free Barcode Generator & GST Billing Label Maker

This free online barcode generator helps Indian retailers, boutiques, kirana stores and small businesses create GST-ready price tags and shelf labels in minutes. Generate CODE128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and CODE39 barcodes, add HSN codes and GST rates, and print professional retail labels on A4, A5, thermal or photo-size paper — all directly in your browser, with no signup or software installation required. Import your entire product catalog from an Excel sheet, CSV file, or a Google Sheets link, and every product you save stays private in your own browser's local storage.

Frequently asked questions

Is this barcode generator free to use?

Yes — it's completely free, runs in your browser, and doesn't require an account.

Which barcode formats are supported?

CODE128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and CODE39, selectable from the Print Labels screen.

Can I import my product list from Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes — upload an XLSX, XLS or CSV file, or paste a public Google Sheets link, and columns map automatically.

Where is my product data stored?

Everything is saved locally in your browser only — nothing is uploaded to a server.

Do I need to install any software?

No — it runs entirely in your browser on desktop or mobile, with nothing to download.

Can I add HSN, GST rate and my GSTIN to labels?

Yes — each product stores its own HSN and GST rate, and your business GSTIN can be set once and shown on every label.

What paper and label sizes can I print on?

A4, A5, US Letter, 4x6 photo paper, 100x150 mm thermal rolls, or any fully custom size you enter in millimetres.

Is there a limit on barcodes or labels I can generate?

No — add unlimited products and print as many label copies as you need, free of charge.

Is this suitable for clothing, footwear or grocery stores?

Yes — size, color, material and gender fields suit apparel and footwear, while HSN and GST fields suit grocery, electronics and general retail.

Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes, the layout is responsive — for precise printing on specific paper sizes, a desktop or laptop browser is recommended.
